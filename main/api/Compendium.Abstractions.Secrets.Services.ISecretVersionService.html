Immutable secret versions. Every read addresses an explicit revision — the port has no "latest" concept, deliberately: which revision is current is the caller's metadata, so history stays stable and rollback is a re-pointing of references, independent of provider-side mutable state.

AccessAsync(SecretVaultConnection, string, long, CancellationToken)

Reads the material of an exact revision. Revisions are immutable, so a successful read is cacheable indefinitely by the caller. Fails with SecretVault.VersionDisabled on a kill-switched revision and SecretVault.VersionNotFound on a destroyed or unknown one.
